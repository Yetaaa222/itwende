How we work

Six clear steps, so clients always know what is next

  1. Enquiry

    Client contacts us by email, LinkedIn or our client line.

  2. Package and deposit

    Client selects a package and pays a 50% deposit.

  3. Plan

    We issue a document checklist or travel plan.

  4. Coordination

    We arrange drivers, accommodation and guides, plus lawyers, accountants and ZDA meetings for investors.

  5. On the ground

    We receive the client on arrival and manage the programme anywhere in Zambia.

  6. Completion

    Client pays the balance and shares a review.

A named advisor for every client, itemised fees with receipts, and a clear service agreement.We coordinate legal work but do not give legal advice.
